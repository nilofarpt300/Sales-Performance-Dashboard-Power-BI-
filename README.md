# Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)
## Executive Summary
An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyse sales performance, profitability, regional trends and team contributions. The project demonstrates how data visualisation and business intelligence can support performance monitoring and data-driven decision-making. 
The dashboard provides an overview of sales performance throughout 2023, reporting approximately £30.31M in sales and £23M in profit, as displayed in the report. It enables stakeholders to compare geographical performance, evaluate sales team contributions and monitor monthly trends to identify potential areas for business improvement.
## Business Problem
The business needed a clear, consolidated view of sales performance across regions, countries, sales representatives and teams. The challenge was to monitor key performance indicators, understand sales  and profitability trends and identify areas for improvement. This project addresses the need for data-driven reporting to support better business decisions and sales performance management.
## Objectives
* Monitor sales revenue and profitability.
* Compare performance across countries and regions.
* Evaluate sales representative and team contributions.
* Identify monthly trends and opportunities for improvement.
## Tools and Technologies
* Power BI - Dashboard development and visualisation.
* Microsoft Excel- Data source and preperation.
* Power Query- Data cleaning and transformation.
* DAX - KPI calculations and measures.
* Data modelling - Relationships between sales, employee, and locationtables.
## Key Performance Indicators
* Total Sales Amount
* Total Profit
* Monthly Sales and Profit trends
* Sales by Country and Region
* Sales Performance by Representative and Team
## Business questions answered
What is the overall sales and profit performance?
Which countries and regions contribute most to sales?
Which sales representatives and teams perform best?
How do sales and profit change month by month?
Where are the opportunities to improve performance?
## Results and Key Business Insights
* Overall performance: The dashboard reports approximately £30.31M in total sales and £23m in total profit.
* Regional performance: APAC leads the regional sales comparison shown in the dashboard.
* Geographical Insights: The USA represents the largest visible share of sales among the countries displayed.
